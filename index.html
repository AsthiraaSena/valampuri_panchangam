Pancha-Angam Foundations

The 5 Sacred Limbs of Time

Pancha-Angam represents the cosmic forces governing health, longevity, prosperity, actions, and fortune.

Tithi (திதி)

The lunar day energy governed by Lord Shiva & Devi, determining divine grace and mental harmony for rituals and new beginnings.

Vara (வாரம்)

The planetary solar day dictating physical vitality, longevity, and optimal mundane activities based on celestial rulership.

Nakshatra (நட்சத்திரம்)

The 27 lunar mansions radiating distinct karmic vibrations, guiding personal destiny, ceremonies, and Muhurtham timing.

Yoga (யோகம்)

The auspicious mathematical synthesis of Sun and Moon longitudes, cleansing obstacles and bestowing spiritual fruitfulness.

Karana (கரணம்)

The half-tithi kinetic force governing the practical execution of contracts, journeys, and enterprise endeavors.
